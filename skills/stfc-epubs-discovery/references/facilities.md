# STFC Facilities and Publication Types Reference

## Core National Facilities & Departments

When filtering ePubs search queries using the `dept` parameter, use these canonical names or keywords:

| Facility / Department Name | Common Code | Description | Key Research Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ISIS Pulsed Neutron and Muon Source** | `ISIS` | National pulsed neutron and muon spallation source at RAL | Condensed matter, chemistry, materials, quantum magnetism, spectroscopy |
| **Diamond Light Source** | `Diamond` | UK national synchrotron light source | Macromolecular crystallography, spectroscopy, imaging, nanoscience |
| **Central Laser Facility (CLF)** | `CLF` | High-power and ultra-fast lasers (Vulcan, Gemini, Artemis) | Plasma physics, laboratory astrophysics, ultrafast dynamics |
| **Rutherford Appleton Laboratory (RAL)** | `RAL` | Main STFC research site in Oxfordshire | Space science, particle physics, computing, engineering |
| **Daresbury Laboratory (DL)** | `DL` | Northern research campus in Cheshire | Accelerator science (ASTeC), supercomputing, nuclear physics |
| **Scientific Computing Department (SCD)** | `SCD` | Advanced computing infrastructure and research software | High-performance computing, computational chemistry, data science |
| **Particle Physics Department (PPD)** | `PPD` | Experimental particle physics (CERN collaborations, LHC) | High-energy physics, dark matter, neutrino physics |
| **UK Astronomy Technology Centre (UK ATC)** | `UK ATC` | Astronomical instrumentation (Edinburgh) | JWST instruments, ground/space telescope optical systems |
| **National Quantum Computing Centre (NQCC)** | `NQCC` | National quantum computing development centre | Quantum algorithms, quantum hardware engineering |
| **Hartree Centre** | `Hartree` | High performance computing and AI industry applications | Digital transformation, AI for science, industrial supercomputing |
| **Boulby Underground Laboratory** | `Boulby` | Deep underground laboratory in North Yorkshire | Dark matter search, astrobiology, low-background testing |
| **Chilbolton Observatory** | `Chilbolton` | Atmospheric and radio radar facility | Meteorology, radio communications, satellite tracking |

## Publication Types in ePubs

| Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Journal Article** | Peer-reviewed academic research papers published in scientific journals. |
| **Conference Paper** | Papers presented at proceedings and conferences. |
| **Technical Report** | STFC facility technical notes, instrumentation manuals, and experiment summaries. |
| **Book Chapter** | Contributions to edited academic volumes. |
| **Book** | Complete scholarly books or monographs. |
| **Thesis** | Doctoral and master's dissertations sponsored or hosted by STFC. |
| **Patent** | Intellectual property filings from STFC inventions. |
| **Dataset** | Formally catalogued experimental datasets and data tracks with DOIs (e.g. `10.5286/edata/...`). |
